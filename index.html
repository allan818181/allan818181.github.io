A multilingual car sales platform for a Japanese used car sales company (Tokyo HQ, East Africa office in Dar es Salaam). A public storefront plus customer, staff and admin portals. I built it end to end and run it on AWS.

Product
Smart search, filters and infinite scroll; customer orders and documents; a staff photo pipeline (ZIP upload, WebP variants, watermarking); admin reports, analytics and SEO controls; 7 languages and 4 currencies.

      
⇪
Delivery
Every push builds a Docker image, pushes it to ECR and deploys through SSM. Migrations run automatically, and a failed health check rolls back to the previous version.
